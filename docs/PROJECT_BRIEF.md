## Product direction and canonical roadmap

Ìtàn evolved from PhytoScan's label scanning and ingredient-comparison concept
into a Nigeria-first TCIM information platform. Everyday Nigerian adults are the
primary audience. Pharmacists, researchers and manufacturers are secondary
audiences. The problem is fragmented plant knowledge and the difficulty of
distinguishing documented practices from experimental findings.

1. Nearby legitimate sellers with independently checked credentials — roadmap.
2. Verified consumer experiences — roadmap.
3. QR/label scanning and regulatory information — local OCR, a curated QR demo,
   and links to official NAFDAC checks. No authenticated package verification.
4. Ingredient identity, context, evidence, interactions and multi-product
   comparison — implemented using curated records and claim-to-evidence analysis.
5. Real-time outbreak awareness and disease-linked plant context — roadmap;
   current page has an archived official advisory. Guava laboratory research is
   kept on its plant record, away from cholera-awareness presentation.
6. Seasonal/endemic awareness and relevant plant information — separate
   malaria/WHO section. No live monitoring.

Plant-photo identification and Ask Ìtàn support these six features. Setup notes
saying the idea and stack were undecided refer to November 2. The participant
subsequently selected this product and Next.js/React/TypeScript architecture.
