## During your presentation, you will have the opportunity to show a **2–3 minute** demo video demonstrating how your solution works and what it does. This video will accompany your explanation in front of the judging panel.

## **Recommended Recording Applications**

* **OBS Studio** (Windows/Mac/Linux) – Free and powerful screen recording software.  
* **Microsoft Clipchamp** (Windows) – Easy-to-use tool with recording and editing capabilities.  
* **QuickTime Player** (Mac) – Simple option for screen recording on macOS.  
* **Free Screen Recording** (freescreenrecording.com) – Very simple and free screen recording option, directly from the browser without installation.  
* **Loom** (Browser-based) – Quick screen and webcam recording.  
* **ScreenPal** (formerly Screencast-O-Matic) – Easy and quick browser tool.

## **Recommended Video Structure**

* ## How your solution works in practice

* ## What its key features are

* ## A brief use case or example scenario (optional)

## **Tips for Good Video Quality**

* Use **720p** resolution – it is sufficient for a clear image and keeps the file size small.  
* Make sure the screen is **clean** and only relevant content is visible (avoid background notifications or open applications).  
* The video does not need to have audio – you will describe it live during your presentation.  
* Keep the video **short and focused** on the key aspects of the application.

## **Video Export and Compression**

* **Recommended file types:** MP4, MOV, or WEBM  
* **Resolution:** 1280x720 (HD). Higher resolution is not necessary – the goal is to keep the file lightweight.  
* **File size:** Ideally under **100MB** for easy submission.  
* **Compression:** If your file is larger, you can compress it using tools like **HandBrake** or **VEED.io**.  
* If you use **OBS** or **Clipchamp**, select settings such as *“Web Optimized”* or *“Compressed”* during export.

## **Submission Instructions**

* When you finish your video, please upload it alongside your presentation to the platform indicated by the organizing team. In case you encounter issues with file size or format, contact our organization's IT team immediately.
