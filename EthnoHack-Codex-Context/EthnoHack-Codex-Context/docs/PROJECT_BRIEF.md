# Project brief

EthnoHack 2026 Track 4: Digital Health & Phytotherapy. The broader vision is a Traditional, Complementary and Integrative Medicine (TCIM) information, verification and accessibility platform, initially focused on medicinal plants, traditional remedies and ethnopharmacology in Nigeria. TCIM is broader than a herbal product scanner; the MVP need not cover all TCIM practices.

## Six canonical features

1. Nearby legitimate herbal medicine sellers: discover outlets and show independently sourced credentials, location and contacts. Registered products do not establish seller authorization.
2. Verified user experiences: moderated personal experiences, verified through a meaningful method such as proof of purchase if actually implemented. Reports are not clinical evidence.
3. QR/barcode and NAFDAC verification: scan a code or label or enter a registration number and compare against available records. A match does not authenticate a physical package; an unmatched record does not prove illegality.
4. Simplified composition: explain declared botanical ingredients, scientific and common names, traditional uses, scientific evidence, warnings and limitations with references. Ingredient findings do not establish finished-product efficacy.
5. Real-time outbreak awareness plus disease-linked medicinal plant information and accessibility: link dated, officially sourced outbreak information and prevention guidance with qualified traditional-use or research information and accessibility. Call snapshots snapshots unless a current feed exists.
6. Seasonal and endemic disease awareness plus relevant medicinal plant information and accessibility: explain ongoing or seasonal disease patterns and prevention with qualified plant information. This differs from outbreak alerts.

## PhytoScan

The project evolved from a teammate’s PhytoScan idea: scanning herbal labels, identifying plants, comparing products and checking documented herb–drug interactions using evidence. The present vision expands beyond scanning into the six features above. Interaction checking and product comparison are not agreed separate features. Interaction information could be considered within feature 4 if validated sources, clinical expertise and a safe design are available; do not claim it is implemented or add a seventh canonical feature.
